Iconic Zebu Breeds

Meet the Most Celebrated Bos Indicus

Each breed unique — heat tolerant, disease resistant, and celebrated for A2 milk.

Gir Saurashtra

The most renowned Bos Indicus breed with distinctive convex forehead and drooping ears. High milk yield (8–12 L/day) and excellent heat tolerance. Originated from Gir forests of Gujarat.

  • A2 Beta-casein milk
  • Superior disease resistance
  • Used in crossbreeding programs
Read breed profile

Sahiwal

Best dairy breed among zebu cattle. Heavy milker (8–14 L/day) with strong immunity. Originates from Montgomery region (Pakistan-India border). Docile temperament.

  • Rich butterfat content
  • Ideal for tropical climates
  • Heat & tick resistant
Breed details

Tharparkar

Dual-purpose breed (milk + draught) from Thar desert. Gray-white coat, excellent walking ability. Known for longevity and adaptability to scarce fodder.

  • Milk: 6–10 L/day
  • Exceptional endurance
  • Resilient to drought
Know more

Kankrej

Graceful breed from Rann of Kutch. Lyre-shaped horns, powerful frame. Used in developing the ‘Brahman’ breed of USA. Good milk yield (6–9 L/day).

  • High fertility rate
  • Excellent draught ability
  • Heat & humidity tolerant
Breed profile

Ongole (Nellore)

Magnificent draft breed from Andhra Pradesh. Heavy muscling, compact body. Prized for beef quality in Brazil. Milk yield moderate (3–6 L/day).

  • Strong disease resistance
  • Ideal for organic systems
  • High libido in bulls
Discover more

Vechur

World’s smallest cattle breed from Kerala. Critically endangered but treasured for high milk fat and medicinal properties. Ideal for smallholder farms.

  • Miniature size (80–90 cm)
  • High A2 beta-casein
  • Low maintenance cost

Genetic Superiority

Why Bos Indicus Thrives in Tropical Climates

Unique hump, dewlap, and thermoregulatory genes make zebu the ultimate sustainable cattle.

Heat Tolerance

Bos Indicus possess sweat glands and a hump that stores fat — allowing them to thrive in 45°C+ without stress.

Natural Resistance

Highly resistant to ticks, parasites, and common diseases like mastitis due to centuries of natural selection.

A2 Milk Power

Most indigenous breeds produce A2 beta-casein protein — easier digestion, ideal for children and elderly.

Low Carbon Hoofprint

Zebu cattle require less water and feed, produce lower methane emissions compared to exotic breeds.

Longevity

Bos Indicus cows often produce milk for 12–15 lactation cycles, nearly 2x longer than crossbreds.

Cultural Heritage

Integral to Indian agriculture, festivals, and ‘Panchagavya’ traditional medicine systems.

Save Our Heritage

Preserving Endangered Bos Indicus Breeds

Several native breeds are critically endangered. Conservation programs by NBAGR, BAIF, and Gaushalas work tirelessly.

Breeds at Risk

Kasaragod, Vechur, Malnad Gidda, Punganur — some populations have fallen below 500. Without intervention, they face extinction.
